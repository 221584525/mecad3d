About MECAD 3D

Mechanical Design & Drafting Expertise

MECAD 3D provides mechanical design and industrial drafting support for businesses that need clear, accurate and well-organized CAD documentation.

Using Autodesk Inventor, services include 3D part modeling, mechanical assemblies, 2D technical drawings and modifications to existing CAD files.

  • 3D CAD Modeling with Autodesk Inventor
  • 2D Industrial and Manufacturing Drawings
  • Mechanical Assemblies and Exploded Views
  • CAD Modifications and Drafting Support
Remote project collaboration for companies, manufacturers, machine shops and engineering teams.
